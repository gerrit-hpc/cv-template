# Questions to Ask the Interviewer

A bank of strong questions to include in the brief's "Questions to ask the interviewer" section. Indexed by stage and calibration. Pick 4–6 per brief.

A strong question:

- Reveals what the interviewer actually thinks (not just what's in the careers page).
- Surfaces information the user can use to evaluate the role.
- Signals seniority and seriousness without being performative.
- Has a follow-up the user can pose after the answer.

A weak question:

- Has an obvious answer already on the website ("what's your tech stack?").
- Sounds rehearsed and generic ("what's the company culture like?" — culture is everything; the question is hollow).
- Is a thinly-disguised opinion the user wants to deliver ("don't you think microservices are over-used?").
- Tests the interviewer rather than learning from them.

Each question below comes with a one-line **listen-for** that the user uses to interpret the answer.

---

## recruiter-screen

- **What does the hiring bar look like for this role? What's a candidate strength that's tipped recent decisions in?**
  *Listen for*: whether the bar is calibrated or vibes-based; whether they can name what's worked recently.

- **What's the team's biggest open question right now — the thing the new hire would help resolve?**
  *Listen for*: whether the recruiter has been briefed on substance, or just a JD.

- **How long has the role been open, and how many candidates are in process?**
  *Listen for*: pipeline depth, urgency, whether the user has competition.

- **What's the timeline you're targeting for a decision?**
  *Listen for*: realistic dates and how they handle competing offers.

- **Is the salary band fixed or banded by level? What's the equity / bonus structure?**
  *Listen for*: whether they'll commit to numbers, or dance.

- **What does success look like for the person who gets hired, six months in?**
  *Listen for*: whether the org has actually defined "success" for this seat.

---

## hiring-manager

- **What does the first 90 days look like for the person who succeeds in this role?**
  *Listen for*: do they have a concrete plan or hand-wave; what they prioritise.

- **What's the team working on right now, and what's the next thing on the roadmap?**
  *Listen for*: roadmap clarity; whether they have priorities or are reactive.

- **What's a hard problem the team has been wrestling with that you wouldn't expect to solve in the first quarter?**
  *Listen for*: technical / organisational maturity; whether they admit hard problems.

- **What's the dynamic between this team and the rest of the org? Who do you collaborate with most, and where does friction usually come from?**
  *Listen for*: org health; whether they're honest about friction.

- **Tell me about the last person who was in this role (or the team's last hire). What worked, what didn't?**
  *Listen for*: vulnerability and learning posture; or evasion.

- **How does this team make decisions? Who has the final call when there's disagreement?**
  *Listen for*: decision rights clarity; whether decisions get made or get stuck.

- **What's a value you've had to defend internally? What did defending it cost?** *(leadership-fit calibration only)*
  *Listen for*: whether the manager has real values, or just stated ones.

---

## technical

- **What's the most painful technical decision the team made in the last year, and would you make it again?**
  *Listen for*: honesty about trade-offs; whether they actually retrospect.

- **Where is the code you'd be most embarrassed to show me, and why is it that way?**
  *Listen for*: tech-debt awareness; willingness to acknowledge it.

- **What does your dev cycle look like — from idea to production? Where's the friction?**
  *Listen for*: actual cycle time; whether they know where it hurts.

- **How does the team handle on-call and production issues? What's the last serious incident, and what came out of the post-mortem?**
  *Listen for*: operational maturity; blameless retro culture.

- **What's the team's relationship with quality — testing, code review, design review? What's mandatory, what's aspirational?**
  *Listen for*: gap between stated and lived quality bar.

- **Where do you think the team's technical bar is strongest? Where is it weakest?**
  *Listen for*: self-awareness; whether they can name weaknesses.

- **What's a technical opinion this team holds that other teams (or the wider industry) would disagree with?**
  *Listen for*: whether the team has a point of view, or follows.

---

## leadership

- **What's a value you've had to defend internally? What did defending it cost?**
  *Listen for*: real values vs stated values; whether they can name a cost.

- **Tell me about a time the leadership team disagreed about something important. How did it resolve?**
  *Listen for*: disagreement health; whether decisions stick.

- **What's the org's relationship with risk right now? Are you in expand mode or efficiency mode?**
  *Listen for*: strategic clarity; whether everyone knows the answer.

- **What's the talent density bar look like for the team I'd be joining? What are you willing to fire for?**
  *Listen for*: rigor on performance; whether they actually manage out.

- **How does information flow in this org? Who knows what's happening at the exec level, and how?**
  *Listen for*: transparency norms; whether the user would be inside or outside the loop.

- **What's a strategic bet you made in the last year that didn't work? What did you do about it?**
  *Listen for*: real strategic decisions and real failure; recovery posture.

- **Where are the political third rails in this org? What can a new leader not touch in the first year?**
  *Listen for*: org honesty; whether they'll name landmines or hide them.

---

## final

- **What concerns, if any, came up during the panel that I should address before you make a decision?**
  *Listen for*: actual concerns; willingness to surface them. This is the most important final-round question.

- **What would have to be true for you to give me an offer? What would have to be true for you to give me your top offer?**
  *Listen for*: where the user stands; what levers remain to negotiate.

- **What's the offer process like from here? Who decides, and on what timeline?**
  *Listen for*: realistic decision timeline; political tells.

- **If I accept, what should I know about <company> that the interview process can't tell me?**
  *Listen for*: lived reality; insider perspective.

- **What's the one piece of advice you'd give me about succeeding here that's not on the careers page?**
  *Listen for*: honest read on the culture; what the interviewer wishes someone had told them.

- **What's the comp package structure — base, equity, bonus, sign-on? Is there room to negotiate, and on which levers?**
  *Listen for*: where flexibility actually lives; what's fixed.

---

## Calibration overrides

- `style: structured-behavioral` / `tone: corporate` → drop "third rails" and "embarrassing code" questions; they read as too pointed. Replace with safer variants like "what's an area the team is actively investing in growing?"
- `tone: startup-scrappy` → keep the pointed questions; in startups, blunt is a virtue.
- `difficulty: junior-screen` → drop "what's the team's technical opinion that the industry disagrees with"; can feel performative from a junior.
- `style: culture-heavy` → add: *"What's the worst thing about working here?"* — direct, unrehearsed, very revealing in mission-driven cultures.

---

## Pacing

Realistic time budget for "Do you have any questions for us?":

- recruiter-screen: 5–10 min, room for 2–3 questions
- hiring-manager: 10–15 min, 3–4 questions
- technical: 5–10 min, 2–3 questions
- leadership: 15–20 min (often the leadership round is more like a peer conversation), 4–5 questions
- final: 15–20 min, 4–6 questions

Brief should pick more than the user can ask; they pick on the fly based on what the interview surfaced.

---

## Always rank in the brief

Sort questions in the brief by **priority** for that stage — the user should ask the top one if they only get to ask one. Mark the top question as `*` so it's visible at a glance.
